Privacy Policy — Tetroshape

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Tetroshape is developed by End Key Studios. This page explains how the app handles your information.

The Short Version

Tetroshape does not collect any personal information. The app has no network access at all — it never sends anything anywhere, and it works exactly the same with your device offline.

What Stays on Your Device

The app saves the following using the operating system's standard local storage:

  • Your high score
  • Your sound effects and music volume settings
  • Your game speed setting
  • Whether colourblind mode is on
  • Whether swipe controls are on
  • Whether you have already seen the tutorial and the one-time hint

This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What We Do Not Do

  • No account, sign-in or registration
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No leaderboards or other online services
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the app introduces anything that handles your data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
