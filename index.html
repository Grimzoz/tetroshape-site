FAQ

How do I play? A glowing shape appears on the board. Stack the falling pieces to fill it. Completing the shape blasts every row it covers.

What is the outlined shape under my piece? That is the landing preview — it shows exactly where the piece will drop. The solid glowing shape is the target you need to fill; the hollow outline is your piece.

Can I change the controls? Yes. The buttons at the bottom are the default. In Settings you can switch on Swipe Controls: drag sideways to move, flick to slide to the wall, tap to rotate, hold down for a soft drop, flick down to drop instantly and flick up to hold a piece.

What does the H button do? It stores your current piece for later — once per piece. Handy when the piece you have does not fit the shape yet.

No sound? Settings has separate Sound Effects and Music sliders. Also check that your device is not on silent.

I have trouble telling the colours apart. Turn on Colorblind Mode in Settings — it switches the pieces to a palette chosen to stay distinguishable, with stronger brightness differences.

Where is my high score saved? On your device only — uninstalling the app removes it.
